# Neukölln Pixel Dungeon

## Product

Build a German-language fork of Shattered Pixel Dungeon. Preserve its cute,
readable pixel art and tactical turn-based rules. Humor combines Monkey Island
dialogue and Goat Simulator absurd consequences with Berlin social satire.

The four playable roles are fixed:
- Expat: mage, incomprehensible foreign-language incantations and startup jargon.
- Alteingesessene: warrior/tank, stubbornness and local knowledge.
- Zugezogene: huntress/ranged, positioning and distance.
- Tourist: rogue, stealth and surprise attacks.

The Spätimann is the buy/sell merchant on floors 6, 11, 16.
Treat Reddit stories as inspiration, not verified claims about real people.
Mechanics must be readable and fair even when fiction is absurd.

## Working Team

- Integration/build coordinator: build files, platform setup, release and QA.
- Gameplay agent: Java mechanics, class selection, enemy behavior and level generation.
- Lore agent: German messages, dialogue, descriptions and consistency with mechanics.
- Art agent: original pixel assets and visual design documentation.

Coordinate ownership before parallel changes. Preserve upstream credits and
GPL license. Keep save data separate from Shattered. Do not claim a feature is
playable until it is integrated and tested. Record incomplete work explicitly.

## Development

Use existing Java/libGDX APIs and asset dimensions. Keep localization placeholders
and formatting intact. Desktop is the first validation target. Check the start
guide for the current build command. Android/iOS are later validation targets.
